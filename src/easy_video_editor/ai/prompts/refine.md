Phase 2: refine highlights.

Inside each candidate (blue) zone, choose the precise best moments and create them as highlight
(yellow) zones with the candidate as parent. Cut cleanly: start just before the action builds and end
right after its peak. Keep each highlight within the project's shot length range; when the music has
a beat, prefer lengths that are whole numbers of beats. One candidate may give several highlights, or
none if it turns out to be weak.

Give priority 3 to must-use moments, 2 to good ones and 1 to fillers, and a score from 0 to 1.
Unlocked highlights from an earlier run may be updated or deleted; locked ones stay.
