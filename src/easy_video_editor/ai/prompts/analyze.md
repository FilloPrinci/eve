Phase 1: find candidate zones.

Mark, in every clip, the regions worth considering for the video: action, speed, impacts, close
calls, overtakes, crashes, spectacular views, anything a viewer would want to see. Create them as
candidate (blue) zones with a short label and a score from 0 to 1.

Aim for a total candidate time of about three to four times the target duration, usually 2 to 15
seconds per zone, spread over the best material rather than concentrated in one place. Unlocked
candidate zones from an earlier run may be updated or deleted; locked ones stay.

A good way to start is get_project_state, then get_candidate_windows and get_features for each clip,
confirming the promising moments with a few frames.
